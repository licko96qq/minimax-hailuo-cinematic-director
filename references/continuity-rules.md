# Continuity Rules

## Continuity anchors

Carry at least three of these across connected shots:

- terrain geometry
- water or road direction
- light direction and time of day
- dominant palette
- weather behavior
- human trace level

## Continuing a real clip

1. Extract the end frame from the previous video.
2. Use it as `first_frame_image`.
3. Describe the new move, not the old frame.
4. Keep the same physical world unless the user wants a deliberate break.

## Anonymous human strategy

When the user wants story without a visible hero:

- use traces in Shot 2
- use a tiny back-facing witness in Shot 3
- avoid faces
- keep the environment dominant

## Review checklist before spending quota

- Does Shot 2 clearly continue Shot 1 geography?
- Does Shot 3 feel like a payoff instead of another middle shot?
- Is the camera plan simple enough to complete in six seconds?
- Is there only one new dramatic beat per shot?

## shot-03 — Witness at the Edge

- Dramatic purpose: payoff
- Story beat: revelation
- Primary subject: an anonymous witness facing the full scale of the world
- New information: the traces resolve into a tiny human presence and emotional meaning
- Camera height: ground-ridge
- Framing: extreme-wide witness composition
- Camera movement: restrained hold with slight environmental motion
- Continuity anchors: same Icelandic world, same dawn light, same black sand and glacier palette
- Difference axes: new subject emphasis, new composition, new emotional function, switch away from anchor lock
- Must not repeat: repeat shot-02 tracking angle, look like another discovery shot
- Input mode: t2v

### Chinese Intent

第三段不能再是第二段的变体。它必须变成一个明确的收束镜头：从远处黑沙山脊或海口附近的高地望向同一片冰川河世界，画面中第一次出现极小的匿名背影或停驻车辆，人物不露正脸，只作为尺度感和情绪锚点。这个镜头的任务不是继续找线索，而是让整段故事得到意义上的落点。

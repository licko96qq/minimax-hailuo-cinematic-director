# Storyboard

## shot-01 — The Beacon

- Dramatic purpose: setup
- Story beat: world-establishment
- Primary subject: an isolated lighthouse on a black rocky coast with a tiny rescue boat in the distance
- New information: the viewer understands the storm, the destination, and the human scale of danger
- Camera height: high-aerial
- Framing: wide-establishing
- Camera movement: slow aerial drift toward the lighthouse
- Continuity anchors: same lighthouse, same storm, same blue-hour light, same rescue boat
- Difference axes: world setup, aerial scale
- Input mode: t2v

### Chinese Intent

第一段是建立镜头：暴风中的海岸、孤立灯塔和远处极小的救援船构成完整世界。重点是危险、距离和尺度感，让观众知道这是一场必须到达灯塔的海上救援故事。

## shot-02 — Through the Breakers

- Dramatic purpose: escalation
- Story beat: struggle
- Primary subject: the rescue boat as it fights through the breakers beneath the looming lighthouse
- New information: the viewer now feels the physical difficulty of reaching shore
- Camera height: water-level-low
- Framing: medium-wide tracking
- Camera movement: fast low oblique tracking alongside the boat
- Continuity anchors: same lighthouse, same storm light, same sea state, same rescue mission
- Difference axes: lower camera height, boat becomes primary subject, kinetic lateral motion, closer danger
- Must not repeat: repeat the distant aerial lighthouse composition, stay only as a landscape overview
- Input mode: t2v

### Chinese Intent

第二段必须明显不同于第一段：镜头压到贴近海面，重点从灯塔转为救援船本身。画面要让观众感到船身如何被浪头拍打、如何顶着雨和风向灯塔下方的狭窄入口冲去。这一段的叙事功能是体感上的危险升级。

## shot-03 — At the Edge of Safety

- Dramatic purpose: payoff
- Story beat: arrival
- Primary subject: a witness view from the lighthouse edge as the rescue boat reaches the cove
- New information: the viewer sees that the journey has almost ended and the emotional meaning resolves
- Camera height: cliffside-high
- Framing: extreme-wide witness composition
- Camera movement: restrained hold with the lighthouse beam and storm slowly easing
- Continuity anchors: same lighthouse, same cove, same blue-hour storm palette, same rescue boat
- Difference axes: new witness viewpoint, new emotional function, broader final composition, motion calms down
- Must not repeat: repeat the water-level struggle shot, behave like another action beat
- Input mode: t2v

### Chinese Intent

第三段不能再继续做动作镜头，而是要变成收束镜头：从灯塔边缘或悬崖高处俯看小船终于进入避风小湾，近处可以有一位匿名灯塔守望者或栏杆剪影，但不要抢主体。重点是从‘危险’转成‘接近安全’，让三段成为一个完整故事。
